## Variables (the 2 × 2 core, per step; `probes/labels.py`)

| | binary | 4-class |
|---|---|---|
| **content** | distractor used: the distractor quantity is an operand of the step (distractor-condition traces; simple, unambiguous steps) | chosen operation + − × ÷ (simple steps) |
| **evaluation** | step correct: error type is "none" (computational steps) | error type: none / planning / binding / arithmetic (computational steps) |

- **Rows:** every parsed step of every S2 greedy trace (both conditions, right and wrong) with a
  gold program and not stopped at 512 tokens, on train or dev templates.
- **(choice)** Steps marked `extra_valid` by the parser (harmless redundant or re-derived steps,
  labelled "planning") are excluded from both evaluation variables, because their label is
  ambiguous.
- **Position:** the step's first token (`step_first`), primary; the step's last token
  (`step_last`), secondary. Residual stream `blocks.L.hook_resid_post`, L = 0..25, from the S4
  cache (float16 storage, float32 compute).

## Shared construction procedure (`probes/fit.py`), identical for every variable

1. **Equal training size:** every variable's train rows are subsampled (class-stratified) to the
   smallest variable's train count (distractor used: 3,957 rows). Seeds 0–4 draw the subsample
   and the fold assignment.
2. **Readouts:** an L2 logistic probe on standardised activations (scaler fitted on training rows
   only; λ = 10^k, k ∈ {−4..2}, C = 1/λ), and a difference-in-means readout in raw residual space
   (binary: projection on μ1 − μ0; 4-class: nearest class mean).
3. **Selection on train only:** template-grouped 5-fold CV. **(choice, cost)** two-stage: the
   layer at λ = 1, then λ on the grid at that layer. The difference-in-means layer is chosen by
   the same CV.
4. **Fit** on all subsampled train rows at the selected layer and λ; **score** every dev row with
   the label defined (not subsampled).
5. **Shuffled-label null:** 100 permutations of the train labels within template groups (seed 0),
   the same fit at the same layer and λ, scored on dev.

## Metrics and criterion

- AUROC (binary) or balanced accuracy (4-class) on dev, with a 95% template-clustered bootstrap
  CI (10,000 resamples over the 16 dev templates). n, templates, ICC, deff and n_eff with every
  number (A16).
- **Readable on dev** if the logistic probe's dev metric is **≥ 0.75** and the template-bootstrap
  CI of (probe − mean of the 100 shuffled probes) **excludes 0** (lower end > 0). Permutation
  p = (1 + #{shuffled ≥ real}) / 101 is reported alongside.
- **Primary:** seed 0. The spread of the dev metric over seeds 0–4 is reported.
- **Also reported:** the difference-in-means readout's dev metric; cosines between the content
  and evaluation difference-in-means directions (binary variables) at every layer; a
  **dev-tuned** layer (the best layer on the dev map of E2), clearly labelled as optimistic,
  because selecting on dev and reporting on dev is biased upward.
- **RQ1 statement:** RQ1's hypothesis needs three content variables; this run builds two (the
  third, bound operand, belongs to E3), so it reports each variable and makes **no RQ1 verdict**.
