# Pre-registration: E2 — readout map over layers and steps, development run

**Status: committed before any E2 data exist.** Written on the user's instruction of 2026-11-26
(DECISIONS D49), from the internal evaluation report (Section 3.3, Table 3.3) and
`configs/experiment.yaml`; **(choice)** marks a conservative choice where they leave one open.

* **Date committed:** 2026-11-26
* **Research question:** RQ2 — when and where do content and evaluation variables become
  readable? Hypothesis: content at the step where it is used; evaluation at or after the first
  content deviation. Unsupported if evaluation's earliest reliable step precedes the first
  content deviation, or the two cannot be separated.
* **Scope: DEVELOPMENT** (train → dev; no test template; automatic labels, S5 kappa pending).

## Readout map

- For each 2 × 2 variable of `prereg/E1.md`, at every layer 0–25 and both positions
  (`step_first`, `step_last`): the logistic probe at E1's selected λ for that variable (seed 0,
  E1's equal-size train subsample), fitted on train, scored on dev **by step position**
  1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6+.
- Each cell: AUROC / balanced accuracy with a template-clustered bootstrap CI (2,000 resamples per
  cell, **(choice, cost)**; 10,000 for the cells named in the comparisons below), and cell n.
  Cells with fewer than 20 rows or a missing class are left empty.
- The best dev layer per variable is reported as E1's "dev-tuned" layer (optimistic, labelled).

## Baselines (on the same dev rows, at E1's selected layer)

1. **Text-only:** TF-IDF (word 1–2-grams, min_df 2) + L2 logistic (λ chosen by the same grouped
   CV) on the text the model has produced **before the read position** (the problem text and the
   earlier steps). **(choice)** This is the text available to the model at `step_first`.
2. **Question-only:** the same on the problem text alone. **(choice)** The plan's question-only
   control reads the activation at the end of the question, which the S4 cache does not hold
   (positions are step_first, step_last, final_answer); the text version is used instead and
   called that.
3. **Logit-margin control:** **(choice)** full-vocabulary entropy was not recorded in S2; the
   recorded top-2 logits are. Features: the top-1 − top-2 logit margin at the read position, and
   the mean and minimum margin over the previous step's tokens; L2 logistic.
- Reported: probe − baseline on dev, with a template-clustered CI (10,000 resamples).

## RQ2 timing test (dev failures)

- **Alarm:** the evaluation/binary probe (E1, seed 0, its selected layer, `step_first`) scores
  P(step incorrect). The threshold τ gives a false-positive rate of 0.10 (`rq2_alarm_fpr`) on the
  steps of **correct train traces** (fixed before dev is scored).
- **Per dev failure with s0:** earliest alarm step a; content deviation step = s0 (parser v4,
  lenient).
- **Paired test:** the alarm rate on steps **before s0** in failed traces, against the alarm rate
  on the steps of correct dev traces at the same step positions (each failed-trace step matched to
  the correct-trace rate at its position). The difference gets a template-clustered CI (10,000).
  - **Supported:** the pre-s0 difference CI includes 0 or lies below 0, **and** the alarm rate at
    s0 and after is above the correct-trace rate (CI excludes 0).
  - **Unsupported (evaluation precedes):** the pre-s0 difference CI lies above 0.
  - **Cannot be separated:** neither holds.
- Also reported: the distribution of a − s0 (before / at / after / no alarm), by population
  (primary, natural).
- **Content at the step where used (descriptive):** the content/binary probe's dev AUROC at steps
  where the distractor is used, against steps of the same traces before its first use.
