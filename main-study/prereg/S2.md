  The clauses are the reviewed table `data/distractor_clauses_reviewed.json` (A17). The

* **Primary population: distractor failures** (plan 3.12.3): a distractor-condition trace scored
  wrong whose clean twin (same instance, same prompt) is scored right.
* **Secondary population: natural failures:** clean-condition traces scored wrong. Each failure

* A distractor-condition failure whose clean twin also failed is in neither population. It is

**Size from the power analysis (A6).** The plan needs at least 213 held-out test failures for
its comparisons after Holm correction. Clustering inflates that by the design effect DEFF, and
the 320-failure target covers a DEFF up to 1.5. So the requirement is
N* = max(320, ⌈213 × DEFF⌉) test failures with s0, from at least 40 test templates.
* Planning values: accuracy **71%** (Gemma-2-2B-IT in M0), and an error step found in **81%** of
  failures (the lower end of M0's 81–92%).
* Instances per template: 40 × 6 prompts × 0.29 × 0.81 = 56.4 test failures with s0 per
  instance, so 320 needs **6**. **8** are kept, as in the original draft. That gives about 557
  test failures, about 451 with s0, which still meets N* for a DEFF up to 2.1. Generation time
  is not expected to require fewer (interim projection in `docs/PROGRESS.md`), so the number is
  not reduced. This is re-checked against the measured estimate.
* 89 templates × 8 instances × 6 prompts × 2 conditions = **8,544 greedy traces** (A14).
* **The paragraphs above size the natural failures** (A6). For the primary population (A14):
  - The pilot's paired rate is 0.1875 (66 of 352 pairs right without the clause and wrong with
    it). At that rate, 8 instances give about **292 primary test failures with s0**.
  - At the planning accuracy × the pilot's rate given a clean pass (0.71 × 0.388 = 0.276), they
    give about 429.
  - The target is that of A15, below; the top-up was decided by A20 (none).
  - **Caveat (A18).** The pilot rate was measured with the draft clauses. In 48 templates those
    named a word that is absent from some instances, and 47 of the 89 drafts were rated
    tempting, against 16 of the final clauses. The primary rate may be lower than the pilot's.
* **Design effect of failure occurrence (A11). Since A15 this value is descriptive only;** the
  target uses the primary-outcome deff (below). Fixed from the pilot before any S2 data:

  **deff = 1 + (m̄ (1 + CV²) − 1) × ICC**

  - ICC is the one-way ANOVA intraclass correlation of the failure indicator (1 = wrong)
    across templates, using the ANOVA estimator's n₀ for unequal group sizes.
  - m̄ and CV are the mean and coefficient of variation of the cluster sizes m_i. m̄ (1 + CV²)
    = Σm²/Σm is Kish's unequal-cluster-size adjustment.
  - m_i is the expected test failures with s0 of template i at the planned sizing: 8 × 6 ×
    0.81 × its pilot failure rate.

  Estimated on the pilot by `scripts/pilot_design_effect.py` (`reports/m1/pilot_design_effect.json`).
  The pilot is the baseline's Gemma-2-2B-IT greedy traces on GSM-Symbolic `main`, `main`
  variant: 88 of the 89 study templates × 4 instances = 352 traces. Template 50 is not in it.

  | Quantity | Value |
  |---|---|
  | pilot accuracy | 0.483 (26 of 88 templates without a failure) |
  | ICC of the failure indicator | **0.586** |
  | m̄, CV, m̄ (1 + CV²) | 28.5, 0.396, 33.0 |
  | **deff** (unequal-size adjusted) | **19.76** (17.14 without the adjustment) |
  | **N\* = max(320, ⌈213 × deff⌉)** | **4,209 test failures with s0** |
  | sensitivity: no-op variant of the pilot | ICC 0.454, deff 15.6 |

  Under A11 this raised the failure target to N\* = 4,209. **A15 makes it descriptive:** it
  measures how failure *occurrence* clusters, not the primary outcomes.
  - Also descriptive, the pilot's paired distractor failures: ICC 0.432, deff 9.97
    (`pilot_design_effect.json`, `paired_distractor_failures`).
* **Design effect for the target (A15), clarified before any S2 data.**
  - **Outcomes:** the deff is that of the primary paired outcomes, clustered by template:
    - E3 gold-value decodability, per primary failure;
    - the paired repair difference, per primary failure.
  - **ICC:** one-way ANOVA across the **train + dev** templates only.
  - **Kish deff:** unequal-size adjusted, at the observed test-template cluster sizes (primary
    test failures with s0). The larger of the two outcomes' deffs is used
    (`harness.power.primary_outcome_deff`).
  - **Target:** N\* = max(320, ⌈213 × deff_primary⌉).
* **Top-up (A20, superseding A15's rule), decided 2026-11-13 before any study data.**
  - **Rule:** instances 9–16 of all 89 templates are generated, in both conditions, if and only
    if the projected total with 16 instances stays within the 25 T4-hour budget. The comparison
    is the budget rule's: both conditions, sampled runs included, × the 1.25 safety factor
    (`harness.estimate.top_up_by_budget`).
  - **Projection:** 20.71 T4-hours, × 1.25 = **25.89 > 25**.
  - **Decision: no top-up.** The study is reported as **underpowered**, with its effective
    sample size (A16).
  - **Reported either way:** the template-clustered deff of the primary outcomes (E3
    decodability; paired repair differences; A15's estimator) next to the failure-rate ICC and
    deff. Neither triggers anything.
* **After S2 (reported):** `pipeline.design_effect` reports the failure-occurrence ICC on the
  train and dev templates and the Kish deff on the observed test failures, for each population.
  These values are descriptive and decide nothing.

## Generation (A2, A3)

* **Engine:** Hugging Face `generate` with the KV cache, float32, eager attention (the Gemma-2
  soft-capping reference), **left padding** with an attention mask, batch size 16 (a hardware
  setting, halved automatically on an out-of-memory). One worker per T4.
* **Pure greedy:** temperature 0, `do_sample=False`, repetition penalty 1.0 (set explicitly,
  never inherited), no top-p or top-k. `is_pure_greedy` is checked before every run.
* **Prompts:** both styles (`marked`, `terse`) × paraphrases 0, 1, 2, wrapped in the model's own
  chat template, exactly one BOS token (asserted).
* **Stop rule (A3):** a trace stops at the end-of-turn token, at **max_new_tokens = 512**, or
  at the **end of its answer line**, whichever comes first. The answer line is a line that
  starts with the answer marker ("Answer:", "Final Answer:", "**Answer:**";
  `parsing.steps._ANSWER_LINE`) and has a number after the marker. It ends at its newline. The
  fraction of traces hitting the cap is reported; truncated traces count as failures, as before.
* **Verification (A2): the TransformerLens forward pass runs only on finished traces**, never
  during generation (`cache/verify_cache.py`). It is the S1b-verified configuration: TL without
  weight processing, batch 1, no padding. The pre-registered equivalence check:
  - **Sample:** at least 200 greedy traces stratified by template, 3 per template drawn with
    seed 0 (89 × 3 = 267), fixed in `s2_eq_sample.json` before the check runs.
  - **Criterion:** teacher-forced TL logits must reproduce the HF greedy tokens on **≥ 0.99** of
    the sample's tokens. This is the Phase 0 / S1b threshold, unchanged.
  - **Also reported:** the **max absolute logit difference** between TL and HF at the HF token
    and the HF runner-up (HF's top-2 logits are recorded during generation), with the mean, the
    99th percentile, the number of traces with any mismatch, and the HF and TL margins at every
    mismatch.
  - **Outcome:** the check is a gate (`verify` stage). If it passes, the same TL pass runs on
    all other traces (`cache` stage), and the reproduction over all traces is also reported. **If
    it fails, generation falls back to TransformerLens greedy decoding** (batch 1, TL KV cache,
    same stop rule; `pipeline fallback`), the HF traces are kept aside as
    `hf_batched_rejected/`, and the report says so. The check is then repeated on the TL traces.
* **Resume:** a run that stops is resumed from its output; a trace is never regenerated. Batch
  composition after a resume can differ from an uninterrupted run, which changes only float32
  rounding; the check covers it.
* **Provenance:** for every trace file the sha256, line count, code commit and stage status
  are written (`status/*.json`, `reports/m1/s2_run_summary_<model>.json`).

## Sampled subset (S3; A5)

* T = 0.7, k = 5, top-p 1, top-k 0, repetition penalty 1.0, the same prompts, stop rule and cap.
* **Only for greedy failures on test templates that belong to a population (A14):** primary
  distractor failures (sampled on the distractor question) and natural failures (sampled on the
  clean question). They are used **solely for the stable vs borderline split** (stable = no sampled run correct; `thresholds.stable_failure`). No other
  analysis uses the sampled runs.
* Seeds: one per batch, from the batch's keys (`batched.batch_seed`), recorded per run.

## Activation cache (S4; A4)

* **In the same TL pass as the verification**: one forward pass per trace serves both.
* **Residual stream only, all layers:** h⁽⁰⁾ = `blocks.0.hook_resid_pre` and
  h⁽ˡ⁾ = `blocks.{l}.hook_resid_post` for l = 0…25, i.e. 27 sites.
* **Only at the pre-registered positions** (`probing.positions`), defined in
  `cache/positions.py`:
  - **step_first (primary):** the token holding a step's first non-blank character;
  - **step_last:** the token holding its last non-blank character;
  - **final_answer:** the token holding the first digit or sign of the extracted final answer.

  Steps are the frozen parser-v4 spans (`parsing.steps.segment`). Positions are taken for
  correct and failed traces. A trace whose decoded prefixes are not prefix-stable gets no
  positions and is counted.
* **Storage:** float16 shards (`resid_w*_*.npy`, [rows, 27, 2304]) with an index
  (`index.jsonl`: trace, kind, step, token, shard, row) and a manifest. Only storage is float16.
  Every computation is float32, and a cast that overflows stops the run.
* **float16 check:** for the first 32 traces of the equivalence sample, float32 copies are kept
  (`ref_fp32/`), and the max absolute and relative error of the float16 copy are reported. The
  same check is repeated on SAE features (`cache/store.fp16_sae_check`) when SAE activations
  are computed.
* **SAE activations are computed later from the stored residuals**, on CPU (a laptop is
  enough), with the Gemma Scope SAEs that passed validation (24/26 layers).
* **Disk:** the total is reported by the `report` stage and projected by `make estimate`.

## Scoring

* `parsing/answers.py` (sha256 `9c857958…`): the last number after the last answer marker, on
  that marker's line, normalised for `$`, commas, spaces and a trailing period. No marker → no
  answer → scored wrong. No fallback to the last number in the text.
* Correct = the extracted answer equals the gold answer exactly (as fractions).
* A random sample of 40 extractions is checked by hand and reported.

## Parsing, gold programs and s0

* **Parser frozen at v4** (commit `e559c46`). sha256 at drafting time:
  `expr.py b29fd48b…`, `steps.py 1fe40471…`, `gold.py 5f956924…`, `answers.py 9c857958…`.
  The rules are listed in `reports/m0/parsing_manual_check_v4.md`.
* **Gold program:** from each GSM-Symbolic solution (`parsing/gold.py`, source
  `gsm_symbolic`). Problems whose gold program is not `ok` are excluded from every step-level
  analysis and counted in the coverage report.
* **Error types:** Algorithm 1 order of precedence (planning, binding, arithmetic, none), with
  the v4 rules: alignment tiers 1–3 consume a gold step, references resolve through tier 4, a step
  that correctly reuses an earlier wrong value is not a new error.
* **Primary s0 = lenient s0:** the first step with an error, skipping steps marked
  `extra_valid` (valid alternative derivations). **Strict s0** (every unmatched step counts, as
  Algorithm 1 is written) is reported alongside as a secondary label. Reason: on the M0 traces,
  strict s0 flagged about 20% of correct traces against 5% for lenient, while both agreed on
  failed traces (81–92%).
* **Ambiguous operand matches** (equal values in several slots) are flagged and excluded from
  the operand variable, but kept for the other variables (plan 3.12.4).
* **Failures without s0** (no computational step, stopped early, answer extraction differs) are
  kept in the failure counts but excluded from step-level variable sets, and are reported by
  cause.

## Acceptance criteria (checked on the 2B traces before E1 starts)

| Check | Criterion | If not met |
|---|---|---|
| Equivalence gate (A2): TL teacher forcing on the stratified sample | ≥ 0.99 of tokens | fall back to TL generation (above), and report it |
| Traces with an extracted answer | ≥ 97% | inspect formats; fix scoring only for a new marker form, as a dated deviation |
| Traces hitting max_new_tokens (512) | ≤ 1% | report; such traces stay scored wrong and are excluded from failure sets |
| Gold program ok (GSM-Symbolic main) | ≥ 80% of problems | report; excluded problems listed |
| False error steps on correct traces (lenient s0) | ≤ 10% | parser fix before E1, as a dated deviation |
| Hand check of 40 random parsed steps (fresh seed) | ≥ 36 / 40 correct | parser fix before E1, as a dated deviation, then a new 40-step check |
| Test templates with a reviewed clause (A17) | ≥ 40 | the `problems` stage refuses; the group decides |
| Primary test failures with s0 (A14, A15) | ≥ N* = max(320, ⌈213 × deff_primary⌉) from ≥ 40 templates | no top-up (A20); reported as underpowered, with n_eff |
| float16 storage (A4) | no overflow; errors reported | an overflow stops the run; storage dtype is decided by the group |

The hand check uses the M0 standard: a step is correct only if operation, operands, result,
aligned gold step and error type are all right. A second group member re-checks 10 of the 40
steps independently, and agreement is reported.

## Compute (A7, A13)

`make estimate` runs a timed smoke test on the Kaggle T4s (64 GSM8K-train problems × 6 prompts,
through the same code) and projects GPU-hours per stage and disk use.

**Over-budget rule (A13), fixed before measuring.** If the projected total (× 1.25 safety factor)
exceeds 25 T4 GPU-hours, `harness.estimate` applies these steps automatically, in order, and
records them:
1. reduce instances per template, never below the power floor (N* test failures with s0 after
   deff, from ≥ 40 test templates);
2. if still over, drop the sampled runs; the stable vs borderline split is then reported as not
   done.

If the projection is still over after both, the run does not start and the group decides.

### Amendments of 2026-11-13, third set of group decisions (before any S2, S3 or S4 data exist)

Recorded in `docs/DECISIONS.md` D40–D42. They change no threshold, random-direction count, CI
procedure or annotation rule, and add no quantised or float16 compute.

* **A18: clause generator and pre-review rewrites (D40).**
  - **Bug:** a partitive clause ("d of the X") contradicts the problem when d is not smaller
    than the count of X in the instance. GSM-Symbolic also varies names, objects, places, days
    and units between instances, so a clause written against instance 1 can name something an
    instance does not have.
  - **Instance-level checks**, on every instance 1–16 of every study template
    (`harness/instance_checks.py`):
    - partitive: d < the count of X stated or implied in the instance (the smallest candidate
      group if ambiguous; a group absent from the instance fails);
    - grounding: no clause word that appears in some instances but not others; a pronoun needs
      a same-gender pronoun in the question; no story word before the problem introduces it.
  - **Rewrites:** 64 clauses, applied before the human review (`PREREVIEW_REWRITES`, each with
    its reason). They comprise:
    - every clause with FAIL or UNSURE on irrelevant, natural or no_hint in the AI pre-screen
      (3, 5, 12, 20, 22, 31, 33, 45, 47, 48, 52, 56, 57, 58, 59, 64, 68, 72, 93, 99);
    - every other clause failing an instance-level check: 57 drafts in all. 32 failed the
      partitive check: in 14 d reached the count of X on some instance (6, 22, 33, 41, 44, 45,
      48, 52, 56, 57, 64, 68, 72, 95), and in the rest X is absent from some instances. 48
      named an instance-specific word, and 4 mentioned a story word too early;
    - three found by reading each clause against its template's variation: 53 and 70 named
      something that varies with a word the automatic check cannot see (a dartboard when the
      game can be basketball; brushes when the painter can be an electrician), and 96 was
      implausible ("the party will last 19 hours").

    The failing partitive clauses became non-partitive.
  - **Rewrite rules:**
    - the answer is unchanged;
    - the clause refers only to entities already introduced or clearly new;
    - it uses the story's tense, gives no hint that the number is irrelevant, and has a
      plausible magnitude for every d in 2–19;
    - it is inserted after the entities it mentions.
  - **Checks** (`scripts/distractor_review.py`):
    - all automated checks plus the instance-level ones pass on 89 × 16 instances;
    - for each rewritten clause, an independent solver (`harness/clause_solvers.py`) gives the
      dataset answer with and without the clause on all 16 instances.
  - **Human review of the final clauses:**
    - The reviewers fill `docs/review/human_review_final.csv`. It shows one instance per
      template, a rewrite flag and the yes/no columns, and no AI verdicts.
    - Then `scripts/review_agreement.py` computes Cohen's kappa per question and applies the
      agreed changes (`docs/review/clause_rewrites.csv`). Each change is re-run through all
      checks on instances 1–16.
    - The review is recorded here before the binding commit.
* **A19: temptation covariate (D41).**
  - Weakly tempting clauses are kept.
  - The AI temptation rating of each template's final clause (pre-screen for kept clauses,
    re-rating for rewritten ones) is stored in `data/distractor_temptation.json` as a covariate.
  - It is never used to exclude templates.
* **A20: top-up by budget (D42).** It supersedes A15's top-up rule.
  - The population of A14 is confirmed unchanged: distractor failures are primary and natural
    failures secondary, both conditions for the same instances.
  - Instances 9–16 would be generated if the projection with them stayed within 25 T4-hours. It
    is 25.89 with the safety factor (20.71 without), so **there is no top-up and the study is
    reported as underpowered**.
  - The template-clustered deff of the primary outcomes is reported next to the failure-rate
    ICC and deff.
